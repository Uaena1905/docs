## 1. What is payout (merchant view)

UgameDeal payout (Payout / fund disbursement): Through the UD API, merchants transfer funds from their platform merchant account directly to a designated payee’s local bank card or e-wallet (players, KOLs, suppliers, and others), without manually operating online banking.

- **Fund flow**: Merchant UD account → payee bank card / local wallet
- **Supported parties**: B2C (individual players / KOLs) | B2B (suppliers / channels)
- **Coverage**: Multi-country local banks + e-wallets (for example, mainstream Southeast Asia / South Asia corridors)
- **Integration**: HTTPS + IP whitelist + parameter signature (MD5 / RSA_SHA256)
- **Modes**: Single payout + batch payout + async callback + order query

## 2. Typical payout scenarios (games / overseas business)

| Scenario | Description | Characteristics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player balance withdrawal / original-path refund | Player rebates from game Web Shop top-ups, or balance withdrawals to a local bank card / wallet | High frequency, small amounts, time-sensitive settlement |
| KOL / streamer / promoter commission payout | Weekly or monthly commission to influencers and affiliates | Batch payout; payout proof required |
| Tournament prizes / campaign rewards | Direct cash prizes for esports winners or check-in lottery rewards | One-time batch; clear remarks |
| Supplier / channel settlement | Settlement payments to local publishers or art vendors | B2B payout; larger amounts; T+N |
| Customer service compensation / mistaken-charge refund | Compensation credited after a verified player complaint | Must link order ID; auditable |

## 3. How to use payout

- **Onboarding**: Contact the UgameDeal business team to enable payout permissions
- **Integration**: Connect via API; single and batch payout are supported
- **Security**: HTTPS encryption + IP whitelist + parameter signature verification
- **Settlement time**: Typically real-time to T+1 business day, depending on channel and region
- **Fees**: Per-transaction fee or percentage-based fee; contact the business team for rates
